03 — About

About

M4MMOTH is an Austin-based DJ playing tech house, bass house and melodic techno. His sets are built on groove and clean, deliberate mixing: tracks that sit together, energy that builds without being forced, and a floor that keeps moving for hours.

He reads the room and adjusts instead of playing to a script. Recent dates include Ferro Cocktail Bar and RUST in Rome, Italy, and shows in Jacksonville, Florida.
